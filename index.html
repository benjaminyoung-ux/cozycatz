Finding us

Directions to Cozy Catz Cattery.

Come along St. Aubin harbour front, passing the old courthouse. Head up the hill, passing the Somerville Hotel, and continue all the way up the steep hill until you reach the yellow line.

Look across the road for the post box in the wall, followed by two granite pillars. The outer pillar has the wooden Cozy Catz sign.

Turn between the two pillars and continue to the bottom. Look out for the big walk with painted cats on it. The cattery is just beyond this on the left.

🐾 Look for the wooden Cozy Catz sign between the two granite pillars.
